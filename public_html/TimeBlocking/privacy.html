Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

At Time Blocking, we believe that your time is your most private asset. This policy outlines our commitment to your data security and privacy.

1. Data Ownership

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. All information you enter into the application remains on your device and in your private iCloud storage. We have no access to your schedules, tasks, or preferences.

2. Use of iCloud

Time Blocking uses Apple's iCloud service to provide seamless synchronization across your devices. This data is encrypted by Apple and is subject to Apple’s privacy policies. We do not maintain any independent servers that host your data.

3. Local Security

The application supports local authentication via FaceID, TouchID, or your device Passcode. This security layer is handled entirely by your device’s operating system and ensures that your schedule remains private even if someone else has access to your unlocked device.

4. Third-Party Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics or tracking pixels in our application or on this website. We believe in providing a pure productivity tool without the noise of surveillance capitalism.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The latest version will always be available on this page.

6. Contact Us

For any questions regarding your privacy, please reach out to us at support@melichan.com.
