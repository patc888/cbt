Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Time Blocking ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Data: We do not collect any personal data that can identify you. We do not require you to create an account or provide an email address to use the app.

Application Data: All data you enter into Time Blocking (tasks, schedules, preferences) is stored locally on your device and synced via Apple's iCloud service. We do not have access to this data.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect your personal information, we do not use it for any purpose. Your application data is used solely to provide the functionality of the app on your devices.

3. Third-Party Services

iCloud: We use Apple's iCloud service to sync your data across your devices. Your use of iCloud is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

Your data remains on your device and in your iCloud account until you delete it or delete the application.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@xeo.com.
