Frequently Asked Questions

How does iCloud sync work?

Time Blocking uses Apple's native iCloud service. Once enabled in your device settings, your schedule will automatically sync between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Can I use templates for different days?

Absolutely. You can create as many templates as you like (e.g., "Work Day", "Weekend", "Vacation") and apply them to any day on your calendar with a single tap.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your data is stored on your own device and in your private iCloud storage. We never see your tasks, schedules, or personal information.
