Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a time block?
Simply open the app, navigate to the "Schedule" tab, and tap the "+" button. You can specify the title, start time, end time, and even add subtasks to break down your work.
What is the "Dashboard" for?
The Dashboard gives you a real-time overview of your day. It shows your completion rate, how many blocks you've finished, and highlights what you should be working on right now.
Is my data private?
Absolutely. Your schedule is stored securely in your private iCloud account and on your local device. We do not have access to your plans, routines, or records. Your privacy is our highest priority.
How do I use routine templates?
In the "Templates" tab, you can create recurring blocks of time for things like "Morning Routine" or "Deep Work." Once created, you can easily add these routines to your schedule whenever you need them.
Does the app sync between my devices?
Yes! TimeBlocking works on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. All your data syncs automatically through iCloud, so your schedule is always with you.
Is there a cost for the app?
TimeBlocking is free to download and try. We offer premium features for power users who want to maximize their productivity.
